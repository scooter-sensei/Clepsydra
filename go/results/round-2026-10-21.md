# Round 2026-10-21: correctness under concurrency, the count ceiling, the flood cliff, ConstantTime at 1 µs

Host: AMD Ryzen 5 4600H (6 cores / 12 threads, 4 MB L3 per CCX), WSL2, 4 KB pages, Go 1.24.7. A DRAM access costs ~150–200 ns here. Earlier rounds ran on a 4-vCPU Xeon with 260 MB of L3, so absolute speeds are not comparable with theirs; speed claims in this round are A/B ratios on this host, and absolute times name it.

The round worked down the targets spreadsheet (`temporal-filter-targets.xlsx`): the per-key count ceiling (row 1), the flood cliff (rows 2, 3, 6) and the ConstantTime pad (rows 12, 13), plus the soak's unflagged low answers, which had to be closed before any claim about `Upper` could stand.

## Bugs found and fixed

Five bugs, each with a test that fails on the code before its fix (`verify/replay.sh fixes`, claims R2). Each could make `Upper` read below the true count without a flag, which the contract forbids.

| 2890196 | A ghost entry spilled by a clock behind the key's last touch | its slice decoded as four old: skipped, then freed | `TestGhostAddAheadOfClock`: 3 of 3 → 0 of 3 (replayed) |

A debugging trace (`verify/experiments/trace.patch`, build tag `tftrace`) recorded every write to one identity, and the first low key's records showed the mechanism each time. Soak campaigns of 60 s runs: 15 of 20 clean at 5524228, 9 of 12 with the handshake alone, 20 of 20 with both fixes, 20 of 20 at f304277 (A12, A16).

## Tried, not adopted

| Idea | Result |
|---|---|
| Prefetching the cuckoo search level by level | the insert tail got worse: 85% load p99 2.4 → 3.5 µs (S38) |
| madvise huge pages for the insert-tail table | within run-to-run noise (S38); on the Xeon's DRAM table it was 11–18% faster (X22), so host-dependent |
| Capping the cuckoo search budget of ConstantTime placements | extra evictions, which shed light histories |
| Placing waiting keys in the pad left over after a call's own work | the leftover depends on the caller's key: 56% correct guesses even at a 3 µs pad. Placement runs before the call's own work |
| Three floors per bucket, re-judged | no effect at the defaults up to 4x (no floor rises); with less overflow memory they halve the cliff (two buckets /2 at 4x: 40.8% → 20.0% turned away). Kept: they cost no entries |

The 2026-10-09 round declined "deferring evictions and relocations off the call path" because a key waiting in the stash would make its next touch take a slow path. b798b1f does defer them, but only in ConstantTime, where every call reads its stash set anyway, so a waiting key costs its next touch nothing extra.

## After the records (2026-10-22)

A fuzzer of the `Upper` contract (`TestFuzzUpper`, claims A18) runs seeded single-goroutine cases: a random configuration (table, stash, overflow and hot sizes, horizon, ConstantTime), load from idle to 64x capacity per horizon, and callers' clocks lagging by up to two units, checked against an exact model on every Count and Touch.

- Its first run was a power check: 343 cases on a tree with 2890196's ghost-clock fix undone, of which 36 failed. Twelve of them were replayed on unmodified 4b31a89, and nine still failed: five with the overflow table off and four with it on. Two causes. With the overflow table off, a key that came back after its history was lost read low without a flag. `Degraded` there covers keys not held, so this is documented behaviour that DESIGN's `Upper` statement had left out (X32; now documented). The other was a real bug in four cases, fixed in e9ceb3b: a key restored from the stash by a caller whose clock was behind its last touch got the older clock as its last touch, and later windows starting between the two read nothing. The trace build showed the mechanism in each case. The soak campaigns had not hit it.
- After the fix: 1.004e10 operations over 8,549 configurations, 2.6e9 answers checked, 0 violations (34 min, 4.9 M ops/s on 12 threads). Row 3 is met.
- Power check: with 2890196's ghost-clock fix undone, it fails 6 of 310 cases in 3.3e8 operations.
- `./test.sh regression --full` passes on the records commit (ee76cab), in a clone with full history.

## Background placement (2026-10-22, second session)

The session began with the checks the first one left at HEAD (dd18c6e): the harness replay (the 47 recorded blocks byte for byte, plus five new ones, 4a8aa22 included), a soak campaign (20 of 20, 10 of 10 with `TF_CT=1`) and a 1e9 fuzz run (0 violations); commit 13d97fd, claims A12, A18.

### Row 11: cuckoo searches off the call path

At 85% load 44% of unseen-key inserts find both lines full. A move costs dependent DRAM misses (~189 ns each, about four in flight on this host), and the deeper ones make the tail: 1.3–3.1% of inserts take over 2 µs (S45). `Config.Background` (off by default) takes the search off the call path. A first touch whose lines are full waits in a small **wait room** (one entry per 128 slots, 0.2 B per slot, allocated on the first wait), where its touches and counts find it. A placer goroutine, which runs while keys wait, places it by path or eviction. A lookup that misses the table reads one bit of its wait-room set.

| | Background off | Background on |
|---|---|---|
| 85% load, inserts over 2 µs | 1.27 / 1.59 / 3.12% | 0.16 / 0.18 / 0.19% |
| 85% load, p99 | 2.1–2.8 µs | 1.01–1.09 µs |
| 90% load, inserts over 2 µs | 9.2–11.0% | 0.21–0.33% |
| 95% load, inserts over 2 µs | 52–54% | 16.0–16.4% |

The host takes the CPU away ~1,070 times a second (S43): a 0.5 µs busy loop timed the same way runs past 2 µs in 0.05–0.15% of calls, and a 1 µs loop in 0.12–0.16% (`TestHostFloor`). So p99.9 ≤ 2 µs can't be shown on this host for an operation near 1 µs; with Background the code's own share over 2 µs is ~0.1% at 85% and 90% load. Costs (S46): with Background off the default paths read 0.94–1.02 of 981cd70; with it on, absent-key lookups 1.08–1.12 (the wait-room bit), the caller's inserts 0.52–0.94.

How it got there (claims E17–E19, A19):

- **First design** (E17): waiting keys in the eviction stash, the placer holding the key's claim stripe for its whole search. p99 at 85% fell to 1.5 µs, but 95% got worse (p99.9 50–68 → 140–152 µs): a first touch on the same stripe (1 in 256) waited out the placer's search. It also allocated the stash at design load, 1.8 B per key, which would break row 5's 20 B per key.
- **Wait room and commit without the stripe**: the key stays in the room during the search, and only the install is guarded. The commit marks the room entry busy, installs the entry's current words and removes it, all counted as a move of the key's stripe.
- **Two races found during development**, both fixed before the commit, and both caught again by the tests on a mutation (A19):
  - A first touch that missed the table, then the room, around a commit inserted the key a second time (a key read 7 of 20 events). `TestStashConcurrentEvictions` under `TF_BG=1` found it. Lookups now check the stripe's move counts across both reads.
  - A touch that found its key's room entry expired (under a lagging clock) left it there, and the placer installed it beside the fresh one. The fuzzer found it (case 54). The touch now removes it.
- **A loss recorded early** (c12f98b, also a fix without Background): the placer places a key at the clock of its last touch, which lags when placement does. With the overflow table off, a history it dropped counted as lost from that stale clock, so a returning key could read low without `Degraded` (fuzz case 303). A loss now counts from the history's newest event.
- **Tried, not adopted**:
  - One move inline (E18): it pays for an 8-line search before deciding to wait.
  - One room lock per set (E19): within noise, for 256 KB of locks.
- 981cd70, found on the way: a ConstantTime key that only waited for a slot was reported `Restored` when its next touch installed it.

Checks on the committed code:
- sanity (now with `TF_BG=1`) and regression pass; the race-detector suites pass plain and with `TF_BG=1`.
- Soak: 10 of 10 runs with `TF_BG=1` (64–70 M counts checked per run) and 10 of 10 plain (57–68 M).
- Fuzz: 1.03e9 operations, 873 cases, 177 of them with Background (the placer at seeded points), 0 violations.
- `TestBackgroundConcurrentCounts`: 3 of 3 runs clean.

## Concurrency (2026-10-22, third and fourth sessions)

The soak and `TestFuzzUpper` give each key one goroutine, so no key was ever read while another goroutine touched it. Four races lived there, all fail-open (unflagged `Upper` below the truth), each now fixed with a deterministic test on a hook and a TLA+ model whose old protocol fails and whose new one is checked exhaustively at small sizes (`verify/tla.sh`, claims A20–A24).

| Commit | Bug | Effect | Test, before → after; model |
|---|---|---|---|
| 15bbc28 | A touch taking a waiting key out of the wait room wasn't counted as a move | a read racing it took the key for absent: Count 0, Upper 0 | `TestReadWhileTouchTakesWaitingKey`: 5 of 5 → 0 of 5; `WaitRoom.tla` (A20) |
| 5e21d7e | A touch 8+ units past a register's unit moved word0, then the register | a second touch in between was aged by the gap or reset away: Upper 1 for 2 events | `TestTouchesRacingAfterGap`: fails at gap 8 → passes gaps 1–30; `RegisterEpoch.tla` (A21) |
| 0c08517 | A relocation's copy was visible before its hand-off | the key was live in two slots: touches counted in one while reads found the other; a copy of words read before an eviction came back and its touches were dropped | `TestReadDuringMoveSeesEveryEvent`, `TestMoveAfterEvictionKeepsTouches`; `Relocate.tla` (A23) |
| 277f9ed | A key's wait took a wait-room way the placer was committing | the placer then removed the newcomer's entry (Upper 0, its next touch afresh) or wrote another key's register into it | `TestWaitBesidePlacementKeepsKey`: 5 of 5 → 0 of 5; `RoomWays.tla` (A24) |

How they were found:
- 15bbc28 by reading the protocol.
- 5e21d7e by the concurrent fuzzer's shared keys (`TestFuzzConcurrent`, 6534f47, A22): 59 failing cases in 3 minutes, none after.
- A23 and A24 by its own keys: 8–10 failing cases in 10 minutes at 6534f47, 2 after A23 (A24's kind and one other). A24's mechanism came from a debugging trace (a scratch build that records every move of a key's history and dumps the failing key's records): the key waited, and its next touch found the set's wait bit set and no entry.

The hidden copy also removed code: an abandoned copy now counted nothing, so the fold that added its counts back, and its hot-entry hand-back, are gone. The eviction/move exclusion stays, but the model shows it now saves wasted moves, not histories. Cost: none measurable (inserts and hits 0.99–1.01x).

Open (A22): after both fixes the concurrent fuzzer still fails about once per 7.5 minutes (30 min at 277f9ed: 4,542 cases, 2.98e9 operations, 4 failing cases), in kinds not yet explained:
- shared keys touched at one tick after their slot went stale (4291: Upper 1 for 5 events);
- a hot entry one short on a shared key (3490);
- a key in two slots of one line with Background (1426);
- an old event missing with Background (4509).

Two more seen before: a hot entry one short under Background (case 1068 after the room fix, with events in the wrong units) and a key's only event gone from table, stash and overflow table (1220). None reproduces on demand: case 3490 ran 1,200 reruns at 277f9ed without failing. Failures concentrate on each goroutine's most-touched keys: pool index 0, in every pool, and the heavy keys (the fuzzer's keys inverted from their hashes). Key 0 (`mix64(0) = 0`) is goroutine 0's index 0, not a special hash. The fuzzer stays out of `./test.sh stress` until these are explained.

Also: batches prefetch their chunk's lines and touch each key from the slot found (22735cd, row 8 met: 9.57 → 11.53 M keys/s at 90% load, S48), with ConstantTime batches doing the same work whatever the table holds (7f242b1).
