# Round 2026-10-21: correctness under concurrency, the count ceiling, the flood cliff, ConstantTime at 1 µs

Host: AMD Ryzen 5 4600H (6 cores / 12 threads, 4 MB L3 per CCX), WSL2, 4 KB pages, Go 1.24.7. A DRAM access costs ~150–200 ns here. Earlier rounds ran on a 4-vCPU Xeon with 260 MB of L3, so absolute speeds are not comparable with theirs; speed claims in this round are A/B ratios on this host, and absolute times name it.

The round worked down the targets spreadsheet (`temporal-filter-targets.xlsx`): the per-key count ceiling (row 1), the flood cliff (rows 2, 3, 6) and the ConstantTime pad (rows 12, 13), plus the soak's unflagged low answers, which had to be closed before any claim about `Upper` could stand.

## Bugs found and fixed

Five bugs, each with a test that fails on the code before its fix (`verify/replay.sh fixes`, claims R2). Each could make `Upper` read below the true count without a flag, which the contract forbids.

| Commit | Bug | Effect | Test, before → after |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6af5480 | A relocation between a lookup's two line scans hid a live key | `Count`/`Contains` answered absent (`Upper` 0, unflagged); ConstantTime inserted the key twice and split its count | `TestReadDuringRelocationFindsKey`: 6 of 10 runs → 0 of 10 (replayed) |
| 642c3ef | A ghost add read the epoch once, outside its retry loop | it decoded a newer entry as four slices old and overwrote a live history | `TestGhostEpochRace`: 10 of 10 → 0 of 10 (replayed) |
| 5524228 | An insert evicted a key's copy or source mid-move | the key's history split in two; one part was lost | `TestEvictionDuringMoveKeepsHistory`: 2 and 1 of 3 events → 3 of 3 (hooks added by the fix) |
| a02fe9b | An evictor that read the source before the move began | as above, through the window 5524228 left | `TestEvictionRacingMoveStart`: 1 of 2 events → 2 of 2. Moves and evictions now count themselves per stripe and check each other (a Dekker handshake on sequentially consistent counters) |
| 2890196 | A ghost entry spilled by a clock behind the key's last touch | its slice decoded as four old: skipped, then freed | `TestGhostAddAheadOfClock`: 3 of 3 → 0 of 3 (replayed) |

The last four commits (three mechanisms) explain the soak's rare unflagged lows (X30). A debugging trace (`verify/experiments/trace.patch`, build tag `tftrace`) recorded every write to one identity, and the first low key's records showed the mechanism each time. Soak campaigns of 60 s runs: 15 of 20 clean at 5524228, 9 of 12 with the handshake alone, 20 of 20 with both fixes, 20 of 20 at f304277 (A12, A16).

## Tried, not adopted

| Idea | Result |
|---|---|
| Prefetching the cuckoo search level by level | the insert tail got worse: 85% load p99 2.4 → 3.5 µs (S38) |
| madvise huge pages for the insert-tail table | within run-to-run noise (S38); on the Xeon's DRAM table it was 11–18% faster (X22), so host-dependent |
| Capping the cuckoo search budget of ConstantTime placements | extra evictions, which shed light histories |
| Placing waiting keys in the pad left over after a call's own work | the leftover depends on the caller's key: 56% correct guesses even at a 3 µs pad. Placement runs before the call's own work |
| Three floors per bucket, re-judged | no effect at the defaults up to 4x (no floor rises); with less overflow memory they halve the cliff (two buckets /2 at 4x: 40.8% → 20.0% turned away). Kept: they cost no entries |

The 2026-10-09 round declined "deferring evictions and relocations off the call path" because a key waiting in the stash would make its next touch take a slow path. b798b1f does defer them, but only in ConstantTime, where every call reads its stash set anyway, so a waiting key costs its next touch nothing extra.

## Replays

- `verify/replay.sh fixes`: all ten cases reproduce (fail with the bug, pass with the fix), including the three new ones (torn lookup, ghost epoch race, ghost clock). The duplicate-slot cases had failed to build because `dup_test.go` used a field older commits lack; it now uses `Waiting()` when present.
- `verify/replay.sh harness`: the 34 recorded blocks reproduce byte for byte; the library commits since f14d3ea add new blocks (3b5a427 and 6c051d0 from 10-09, then this round's). The replay used to watch only `filter.go` and `register.go`, so it skipped commits that changed only `ghost.go` or `hot.go` (642c3ef, 2890196, 76b5b9a). It now watches every library file; those three blocks are byte-identical to the blocks before them, as the commits said, and two full replays agree byte for byte (X31).
- Soak at 4b31a89 (60 s runs): 20 of 20 clean, and 10 of 10 with `TF_CT=1`; 20–56 M counts checked per plain run, 16–21 M with `TF_CT=1`. In the plain runs 19–64% of the checked answers were `Saturated` (excluded from the check), probably because the soak's 192 heavy keys (8 per worker) outnumber the 37 hot entries of its 4,096-key filter (not checked).

## After the records (2026-10-22)

A fuzzer of the `Upper` contract (`TestFuzzUpper`, claims A18) runs seeded single-goroutine cases: a random configuration (table, stash, overflow and hot sizes, horizon, ConstantTime), load from idle to 64x capacity per horizon, and callers' clocks lagging by up to two units, checked against an exact model on every Count and Touch.

- Its first run was a power check: 343 cases on a tree with 2890196's ghost-clock fix undone, of which 36 failed. Twelve of them were replayed on unmodified 4b31a89, and nine still failed: five with the overflow table off and four with it on. Two causes. With the overflow table off, a key that came back after its history was lost read low without a flag. `Degraded` there covers keys not held, so this is documented behaviour that DESIGN's `Upper` statement had left out (X32; now documented). The other was a real bug in four cases, fixed in e9ceb3b: a key restored from the stash by a caller whose clock was behind its last touch got the older clock as its last touch, and later windows starting between the two read nothing. The trace build showed the mechanism in each case. The soak campaigns had not hit it.
- After the fix: 1.004e10 operations over 8,549 configurations, 2.6e9 answers checked, 0 violations (34 min, 4.9 M ops/s on 12 threads). Row 3 is met.
- Power check: with 2890196's ghost-clock fix undone, it fails 6 of 310 cases in 3.3e8 operations.
- `./test.sh regression --full` passes on the records commit (ee76cab), in a clone with full history.

## Timing and accuracy (2026-10-22)

- **Row 13**: a pad ending at a fixed time ends at a phase of the spin's clock reads, which follows the call's work. Raw samples show seen and unseen calls ending in four 10 ns clock steps across one 37 ns spin period, in different proportions. A random end over 128 ns brings Count at a fixed 1 µs pad from D 0.019–0.041 to 0.007–0.014, and Touch at 1 µs reads 50.4–50.9% (5b58d63, S41). Met.
- **Row 7**: counting the newest bucket's last event whole gives 0.0807 → 0.0779 (395f738). `TestVelocityBound` puts the floor for this register at ~0.058 under a uniform model; 0.05 needs ~1.25 s resolution near now, more than 48 bits hold (D18). Still a gap, now with its cause measured.
- **Row 11**: this host keeps ~4 misses in flight with 4 KB pages (7 with huge pages) and takes the CPU away ~1,070 times a second for 15 µs at the median (S42, S43). Prefetching a whole search level is slower; one candidate's four lines at a time is faster for deep searches (90% p99 4.4–4.9 → 3.8–3.9 µs). At 85% load about 1% of inserts still take over 2 µs (2-move inserts), so p99.9 ≤ 2 µs needs those off the call path. Still a gap.
