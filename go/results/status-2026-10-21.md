## Decisions

**Method**
- One change per commit, each with a test that fails before and passes after.
- Deterministic records are regenerated only for deliberate behaviour changes, and the commit says which.
- Historical experiments keep the configuration they were recorded with.
- Race tests, repeated runs and a soak campaign are required before closing a concurrency claim.
- Speed is measured as A/B ratios on one host; absolute times are reported with the host named.

**Host and toolchain**
- Ryzen 5 4600H, 6 cores / 12 threads, 4 MB L3 per CCX, WSL2, 4 KB pages. A DRAM access costs about 150–200 ns.
- The targets came from a Xeon with 260 MB L3, so absolute targets are not comparable.
- Go 1.24.7 is at `~/sdk/go1.24.7`. It is not on the non-interactive PATH; the session runner script set it.
- For `-race`: `CC=~/bin/zcc CGO_ENABLED=1 GOFLAGS=-ldflags=-linkmode=external`.
- `core.autocrlf=false` is set.

**Defaults changed this round**

| Setting | Before | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Ghost overflow table | one bucket per history, one floor, slots/4 units | two buckets, three 7-bit floors per bucket (picked by fingerprint), 5 units per 9 slots |
| Stash | slots/8, allocated in `New` | slots/16, allocated on the first eviction (prefaulted) |
| Hot entries | none | slots/128, allocated on first promotion; lock-free same-unit add |
| ConstantTime inserts | cuckoo search on the call path | wait in the stash; placed before each call's own work, within the pad |

**Declined or reverted (negative results to record)**
- Level-by-level prefetch of the cuckoo search: the tail got worse (85% load p99 2.4 → 3.5 µs).
- madvise huge pages: within run-to-run noise.
- Capping the placement search budget: it caused extra evictions and shed light histories.
- Placing in the pad left over after a call's own work: it leaked 56% even at 3 µs.
