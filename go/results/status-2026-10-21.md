- `-race` needs `CGO_ENABLED=1 CC=$HOME/bin/zcc GOFLAGS=-ldflags=-linkmode=external`.

- A queue waits on `pgrep -f "[q]N.sh"`, which any process whose command line contains `qN.sh` satisfies, including the parent shell of a `;` chain that names it: chain such queues instead of waiting.
