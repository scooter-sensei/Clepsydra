## Decisions

**Method** (unchanged from the last note; additions marked)
- One change per commit, each with a test that fails before and passes after (performance changes: an interleaved A/B instead).
- Deterministic records are regenerated only for deliberate behaviour changes. This session regenerated none: the batch keeps its order, and the two race fixes don't change single-threaded runs (seeds 1–80 of the fuzzer replay byte-identical; `./test.sh regression` passes).
- Race tests, repeated runs and a soak campaign before closing a concurrency claim; a new concurrency mechanism also gets a mutation check (undo each guard, show a test or model fails).
- Speed as interleaved A/B ratios on one host, measured in a program that imports the library alone. *Added:* an A/B whose differences are a few percent gets a layout control arm (an unused function added to base); here it read 0.98–1.03x.
- *Added:* a protocol change on the slot words gets a TLA+ model checked with TLC (exhaustive at small sizes), the unfixed protocol shown to fail it and each guard of the fix shown necessary.
- *Added:* a concurrency claim must hold for keys read and touched by several goroutines at once. The soak and the seeded fuzzer give each key one owner, which hid two races this session.

**Taken this session**
- Batches keep their order (an earlier port reordered keys; dropped). `warm` prefetches the chunk's lines; each touch starts from the slot `warm` found, re-checked. Chunks are 32 keys. In ConstantTime, `warm` prefetches both lines and looks nothing up (security review finding).
- Wait-room fix: a touch that takes a waiting key out of the room counts as a move of its stripe.
- Epoch fix, uncommitted, recommended to commit:
  - A touch 8 or more units past the register's unit moves word0 and the register together under word1 busy (`realign`, counted as a move).
  - Other touches stay lock-free, and `bump` reads the register against word0 as it is then.
  - A touch that finds word1 busy waits.
  - `slotWords` re-reads word0, `relocate` won't copy a busy or changed pair, `fold` holds word1, `markSat` won't clear the saturated bit under a busy word1.
  - It costs +5% on touches in the same unit and with gaps under 8 units, +12% (single goroutine) and +23% (4 goroutines) on touches after 8 or more idle units. Accepted as the price of closing a fail-open race.
- Residual not fixed: hot-entry demotion writes a register at the demoting touch's unit. A concurrent touch that moved word0 8 or more units further between the two would leave them misaligned. It needs a recently hot key with an 8-unit jump in a nanosecond window; documented, not handled.
