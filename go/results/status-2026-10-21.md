## Decisions and method (additions)

- **A hold of an occupied slot always counts as a move of the occupant's stripe** (realign, relocation, eviction, the sweep, inserts over expired entries). A lookup that misses during a hold then looks again instead of inserting a second copy. DESIGN states it.
- **A layout control must be linked code.** An uncalled function is dropped by the linker; the arm then measures identical programs (X33). Use a function called once from `New`. On this host a 32-byte change moves TouchAndCount's importer loop ~3% (S49), so speed claims under ~3% on hit-only loops need such a control.
- When a fix's guard needs conditions the fuzzer can't make (A26's second guard, A27), say so, and let the hook tests carry the evidence; the straggler mode was the attempt to give the fuzzer power there, and it has none.
